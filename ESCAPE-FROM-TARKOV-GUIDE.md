## What is Escape from Tarkov?

**Escape from Tarkov (EFT)** is a hardcore tactical first-person shooter developed by Battlestate Games. It combines elements of:
- **Realistic combat simulation** with detailed weapon mechanics
- **MMO-style progression** with character skills and quests
- **Survival mechanics** requiring food, water, and medical attention
- **High-stakes gameplay** where you lose everything you bring if you die
- **Economic systems** with traders, flea market, and crafting

### Key Features
- **Raids**: Enter a map, complete objectives, loot items, and extract before dying
- **Permadeath items**: Die and you lose everything in your inventory (except secure container items)
- **Realistic gunplay**: Weapon modifications, bullet ballistics, armor penetration
- **Player-driven economy**: Buy, sell, and trade with other players
- **Persistent progression**: Level up skills, traders, and your hideout

## Getting Started

### First Steps After Installation

#### 1. Choose Your Edition
Tarkov has several editions (Standard, Left Behind, Prepare for Escape, Edge of Darkness). Higher editions provide:
- Larger stash size (storage capacity)
- Bigger secure container (keeps items safe when you die)
- Starting gear and currency
- Season pass for future DLCs

**Recommendation**: Start with Standard Edition to see if you enjoy the game before upgrading.

#### 2. Complete the Tutorial
- Play the offline tutorial to learn basic controls
- Practice shooting at the shooting range
- Familiarize yourself with inventory management

#### 3. Learn the Stash System
Your stash is where all your items are stored between raids:
- **Organize efficiently**: Use containers (weapon cases, item cases)
- **Sort by type**: Weapons, ammo, medical, food, quest items
- **Keep backup gear**: Always have 2-3 loadouts ready

#### 4. Understand Your PMC vs Scav
- **PMC (Private Military Company)**: Your main character who levels up and progresses
- **Scav (Scavenger)**: Free randomized loadout character with no risk (20-minute cooldown)

**Pro Tip**: Use Scav runs to learn maps and earn money risk-free!

## Core Game Mechanics

### The Raid Cycle

1. **Pre-Raid Preparation**
   - Select PMC or Scav
   - Choose map and time of day
   - Equip weapons, armor, ammo, medical items
   - Bring specific items for quests
   - Check insurance (PMC only)

2. **During Raid**
   - Spawn at a random (or selected) location
   - Complete objectives (kill players, loot, quests)
   - Manage health, stamina, and inventory
   - Navigate to extraction point
   - Survive AI Scavs, player Scavs, and other PMCs

3. **Extraction**
   - Reach designated extract point within time limit
   - Some extracts have requirements (money, no backpack, etc.)
   - Wait for extraction timer (3-10 seconds)
   - Successfully extract to keep all your loot

4. **Post-Raid**
   - Organize your stash
   - Turn in quests
   - Heal your PMC
   - Sell or use looted items
   - Prepare for next raid

### Health System

**Body Parts**: Head, Thorax, Stomach, Arms (L/R), Legs (L/R)
- **Head/Thorax at 0 HP = Instant death**
- Damaged limbs cause penalties (aim shake, reduced speed, etc.)
- Blacked limbs take damage to other parts when hit further

**Status Effects**:
- **Bleeding (Light/Heavy)**: Drains HP over time - use bandages/tourniquets
- **Fracture**: Reduced movement/aiming - use splints or surgical kits
- **Pain**: Screen blur and tremors - use painkillers
- **Contusion**: From explosions - temporary stun
- **Dehydration/Exhaustion**: Drains HP - drink water/eat food

**Medical Items**:
- **AI-2 Medikit**: Cheap, basic healing
- **CAR First Aid Kit**: Better healing, stops light bleeds
- **IFAK**: Fast healing, stops heavy bleeds
- **Salewa**: Versatile healing item
- **CMS Kit/Surv12**: Restores blacked limbs (emergency use)
- **Grizzly**: High-capacity healing

### Stamina and Weight

- **Stamina**: Used for sprinting, jumping, holding breath (aiming)
- **Weight System**: Overweight = reduced stamina and movement speed
  - 0-20kg: No penalties
  - 20-40kg: Minor penalties
  - 40kg+: Severe movement restrictions

**Weight Management Tips**:
- Drop unnecessary items in raid
- Use backpack-in-backpack stacking for looting
- Prioritize high-value, low-weight items
- Level Strength skill to increase capacity

### Insurance

- **Prapor Insurance**: Cheap, 24-36 hour return time
- **Therapist Insurance**: Expensive, 12-24 hour return time

**How it works**:
- Insure items before raid
- If you die and another player doesn't loot your insured items, you get them back
- Hide insured gear in bushes before extracting with better loot

**Pro Tip**: Always insure! Even if you die 70% of the time, the 30% returns pay for themselves.

## Maps and Locations

### Beginner-Friendly Maps

#### 1. **Woods**
- **Size**: Medium
- **Difficulty**: Easy-Medium
- **Best for**: Scav runs, learning extracts, quest items
- **Key features**:
  - Open terrain with forest cover
  - Lumber Mill (high PvP area)
  - Sniper rock (avoid early game)
  - Good for learning long-range combat

**Extracts**: ZB-014, ZB-016, Outskirts, UN Roadblock (near vehicle extract)

#### 2. **Customs**
- **Size**: Medium
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Best for**: Early quests (most starter quests are here)
- **Key features**:
  - Dorms (2-story and 3-story buildings with good loot)
  - Gas station
  - Construction site
  - Linear map layout (easier navigation)

**Extracts**: Crossroads, Trailer Park, RUAF Roadblock, ZB-1011/1012

**Warning**: High traffic area due to early quests - expect PvP!

#### 3. **Factory**
- **Size**: Tiny (indoor)
- **Difficulty**: HARD (PvP focused)
- **Best for**: Quick combat practice, Scav runs for fast loot
- **Key features**:
  - Smallest map (5-10 minute raids)
  - Constant PvP combat
  - Close-quarters battle training
  - Office area (good loot, dangerous)

**Extracts**: Gate 3, Gate 0, Cellars (PMC), Office Window (Scav)

**Recommendation**: Avoid as beginner PMC, fine for Scav runs.

### Intermediate Maps

#### 4. **Shoreline**
- **Size**: Large
- **Difficulty**: Medium-Hard
- **Best for**: Resort loot runs, Therapist quests
- **Key features**:
  - Health Resort (3 buildings with high-tier loot)
  - Long-range combat areas
  - Coastal village
  - Weather station

**Extracts**: Road to Customs, Tunnel, CCP Temporary, Lighthouse (conditional)

#### 5. **Interchange**
- **Size**: Large (indoor mall)
- **Difficulty**: Medium-Hard
- **Best for**: Tech loot, Ragman quests, learning indoor combat
- **Key features**:
  - Massive ULTRA shopping mall
  - KIBA gun store (locked, requires keys)
  - Tech stores (high-value loot)
  - Underground parking garage
  - Scav boss Killa (extremely dangerous)

**Extracts**: Railway Exfil, Emercom Checkpoint, Power Station (Scav)

#### 6. **Reserve**
- **Size**: Medium-Large
- **Difficulty**: Hard
- **Best for**: Military loot, raider farming
- **Key features**:
  - Underground bunkers
  - Military base
  - Raiders (AI with great gear)
  - Hermetic bunker extract (requires D-2 power activation)
  - Scav boss Gluhar and guards

**Extracts**: D-2, Sewer/Bunker extract, Cliff Descent (paracord + red rebel)

### Advanced Maps

#### 7. **The Lab** (Requires Keycard)
- **Size**: Medium (indoor research facility)
- **Difficulty**: EXTREME
- **Best for**: High-tier loot, endgame content
- **Key features**:
  - Requires "Lab access keycard" to enter (~250k roubles)
  - Raiders spawn constantly
  - Best loot in the game
  - No Scav runs allowed
  - High PvP with geared players

#### 8. **Lighthouse**
- **Size**: Very Large
- **Difficulty**: Hard
- **Best for**: Rogue farming, high-tier loot
- **Key features**:
  - USEC Rogue AI guards (KOS on PMCs, sometimes friendly to USEC)
  - Water treatment plant
  - Lighthouse area
  - Chalet (high loot)
  - Long-range combat

#### 9. **Streets of Tarkov**
- **Size**: Massive (urban streets)
- **Difficulty**: Hard-EXTREME
- **Best for**: Urban combat, variety of loot
- **Key features**:
  - Newest map (most demanding on PC)
  - Complex layout with many buildings
  - Scav boss Kaban and guards
  - High performance requirements

### Map Learning Strategy

1. **Start Offline**: Play maps offline with PvE enabled to learn layouts
2. **Use Maps**: Keep a second monitor or phone with online maps (mapgenie.io/tarkov)
3. **Memorize Extracts**: Know at least 2 routes to extraction
4. **Learn Landmarks**: Use buildings and terrain features for navigation
5. **One at a time**: Master 1-2 maps before expanding

## Combat and Gunplay

### Weapon Basics

#### Weapon Types
1. **Assault Rifles** (AK-74, M4A1, HK416)
   - Versatile, medium-range combat
   - Good ammo variety
   - Highly modifiable

2. **SMGs** (MP5, MPX, Vector)
   - Close-quarters combat
   - High fire rate, low recoil
   - Cheaper than rifles
   - Weaker against armor

3. **DMRs/Battle Rifles** (ADAR, TX-15, SR-25)
   - Long-range precision
   - Semi-automatic
   - High per-shot damage

4. **Bolt-Action Rifles** (Mosin, M700, DVL-10)
   - Extreme range
   - One-shot potential
   - Slow fire rate

5. **Shotguns** (MP-153, Saiga-12)
   - Devastating at close range
   - Budget option with AP-20 slugs
   - Limited range

6. **Pistols** (Glock, P226, Five-seveN)
   - Backup/starter weapon
   - Limited effectiveness
   - Can headshot kill if lucky

#### Recommended Starter Weapons

**Budget Tier** (< 50k roubles):
- **SKS**: Cheap semi-auto rifle, PS ammo decent early game
- **VPO-136**: Cheap AK variant, uses 7.62x39 ammo
- **PP-19 Vityaz**: Budget SMG with high capacity magazines
- **MP-153 Shotgun**: With AP-20 slugs can kill armored players

**Mid-Tier** (50-150k roubles):
- **AK-74M/N**: Reliable 5.45x39 rifle, good mods available
- **RFB**: .308 rifle, high damage, compact
- **ADAR 2-15**: 5.56 rifle, cheap M4 alternative

**High-Tier** (150k+ roubles):
- **M4A1/HK416**: Best 5.56 platforms, highly customizable
- **Mutant (MK-47)**: 7.62x39 rifle with high fire rate
- **SR-25**: .308 DMR, excellent range and damage

### Ammunition Guide

**Ammo is MORE important than the gun!** A cheap gun with good ammo > expensive gun with bad ammo.

#### Ammo Stats to Know
- **Penetration**: Ability to pierce armor
- **Damage**: HP damage dealt
- **Armor Damage %**: How much it damages armor

#### Best Ammo by Caliber (Ranked)

**7.62x39 (AKM, SKS)**:
- Best: BP (go-to for PvP)
- Good: PS (budget option)
- Avoid: HP, US

**5.45x39 (AK-74)**:
- Best: BS, Igolnik (high pen)
- Good: BT, BP
- Budget: PP

**5.56x45 (M4, ADAR)**:
- Best: M995 (best pen)
- Great: M855A1 (balanced)
- Budget: M856A1

**7.62x51 (.308 NATO)**:
- Best: M61, M62
- Good: M80
- Budget: M80 (actually quite good)

**9x19 (MP5, MPX)**:
- Best: AP 6.3
- Good: PBP, Quakemaker
- Avoid: PSO, Pst gzh against armor

**12 Gauge (Shotguns)**:
- Best: AP-20 slugs (can pen armor)
- Leg meta: Magnum Buckshot (aim for legs)

#### Understanding Armor Classes

Armor ranges from **Class 1-6**:
- **Class 1-2**: Protects against pistols and buckshot only
- **Class 3**: Stops most pistol rounds, some rifle rounds
- **Class 4**: Mid-tier protection (common mid-game)
- **Class 5**: High-tier protection (expensive)
- **Class 6**: Best protection (very expensive, endgame)

**Ammo Penetration Rule**:
- Use high-pen ammo (40+) against geared players
- Use high-damage ammo against unarmored/low-armor targets
- Leg meta (high damage, low pen) can work against heavy armor

### Weapon Modding

**Key Mod Stats**:
1. **Recoil**: Lower = easier to control
2. **Ergonomics**: Higher = faster ADS, less stamina drain
3. **Accuracy/MOA**: Lower MOA = tighter grouping at range

**Essential Mods for Beginners**:
- **Optics**: Red dots for close-range, magnified for long-range
- **Foregrip**: Reduces recoil
- **Stock**: Reduces recoil
- **Muzzle Device**: Flash hider or compensator
- **Laser/Flashlight**: Improves hipfire accuracy

**Budget Modding**:
- Focus on recoil reduction first
- Skip expensive cosmetic mods
- Use Preset builder in Inspect window to plan mods
- Check Flea Market for cheaper alternatives

**Preset System**:
- Save your favorite builds in Weapon Presets
- Purchase all mods at once with "Auto-assemble"
- Share presets with friends

### Combat Tactics

#### Movement
- **Never stand still**: Strafe, peek, reposition
- **Sound discipline**: Walking and slow crouching are silent
- **Use cover**: Always have something to duck behind
- **Angle advantage**: Peek from different positions each time

#### Positioning
- **High ground**: Huge advantage in Tarkov
- **Avoid silhouettes**: Don't stand on skylines or in doorways
- **Control sight lines**: Limit enemy angles on you
- **Use shadows**: Harder to spot in dark areas

#### Engagement Distance
- **0-15m**: SMGs, shotguns excel; use hipfire
- **15-50m**: Assault rifles optimal range
- **50-100m**: DMRs and low-recoil rifles
- **100m+**: Bolt actions and magnified optics

#### Fighting Tips
1. **Aim for the head**: Bypasses armor (except helmets)
2. **Burst fire**: 3-5 round bursts for accuracy
3. **Recoil control**: Pull down while spraying
4. **Lean shooting**: Use Q/E to lean around cover
5. **Blind fire**: Use to suppress without exposing yourself
6. **Pre-fire**: Shoot where you expect enemies
7. **Audio cues**: Listen for footsteps, reloads, meds

#### When to Engage vs Avoid
**Engage when**:
- You have positional advantage
- You have surprise
- You're confident in your aim
- Quest requires PMC kills

**Avoid when**:
- Heavily outgeared
- Low on ammo/meds
- Already have valuable loot
- Outnumbered

**Retreat strategy**:
- Use grenades to cover retreat
- Break line of sight, reposition
- Don't peek the same angle twice
- Move to extraction via alternate route

## Looting and Economy

### What to Loot (Priority Order)

#### High Priority (Always take if you have space)
1. **Keys**: Required for locked rooms, some worth millions
2. **Graphics cards**: 300k-500k value
3. **LEDx, REAP-IR, Thermal scopes**: 500k-2M+ value
4. **Bitcoin, Goldchains, Rolers**: 300k+ value
5. **Intelligence folders, SSD drives, Tetris**: 200k-500k
6. **Quest items**: Essential for progression
7. **High-tier ammo**: 1-3k per round

#### Medium Priority
- Weapon mods (suppressors, sights)
- Valuable barter items (bolts, nuts, fuel)
- Mid-tier armor and helmets
- Meds and stims
- Modded weapons

#### Low Priority (Only if space available)
- Unmodded weapons
- Low-tier armor
- Basic foods and drinks
- Common ammo

#### Never Loot
- Damaged/broken items (red durability)
- Heavy, low-value items (buckshot, cheap gear)
- Items worth less than 10k per slot

### Money Making Methods

#### 1. Scav Runs (Zero Risk)
- Run every 20 minutes
- Loot stashes and hidden caches
- Extract with anything you find
- Expected profit: 100-500k per run
- **Best maps**: Woods, Shoreline, Interchange

#### 2. Loot Running (Low Risk)
**Hidden Stashes**:
- Blue barrels hidden around maps
- Contain random loot
- Learn stash routes on Woods/Customs/Shoreline
- Bring a big backpack
- Avoid high-traffic areas

**Recommended Route**: Woods stash run (15+ stashes, low PvP)

#### 3. Quest Completion
- Unlocks trader levels
- Provides cash rewards
- Opens better gear for purchase
- Some quests give rare items

#### 4. Flea Market Flipping
- Unlocks at Level 15
- Buy low, sell high
- Craft items in Hideout, sell for profit
- **Profitable crafts**: Water filters, scopes, ammo

#### 5. High-Risk Runs
- Rush high-loot areas (Resort, Ultra Mall, Reserve bunkers)
- High PvP, high reward
- Need good gear and map knowledge

### Traders and Reputation

#### The Traders

1. **Prapor** (Weapons, ammo, armor)
   - Level 1: Basic AKs and ammo
   - Level 2: Better ammo and armor
   - Level 3-4: High-tier gear

2. **Therapist** (Meds, food, quests)
   - Healing services
   - Most quests in early game
   - Container sales

3. **Fence** (Random items, player-sold gear)
   - Lowest prices when selling
   - Random inventory that refreshes
   - Scav karma related

4. **Skier** (Weapons, mods, western gear)
   - M4 platforms
   - Weapon attachments
   - Some early quests

5. **Peacekeeper** (Western weapons, requires USD)
   - M4A1, HK416
   - Best 5.56 ammo
   - Uses US Dollars instead of Roubles

6. **Mechanic** (Weapon mods, high-tier gear)
   - Best weapon attachments
   - High-tier ammo
   - Weapon maintenance

7. **Ragman** (Armor, rigs, backpacks)
   - All clothing and armor
   - Backpacks and rigs
   - Armored rigs

8. **Jaeger** (Hunting gear, consumables)
   - Bolt-action rifles
   - Survival items
   - Difficult quests (often frustrating)

#### Leveling Traders
Requirements for each level:
- **Player Level**: Must be high enough
- **Reputation**: Complete quests for that trader
- **Money Spent**: Spend roubles buying from trader

**Pro Tip**: Don't sell to Fence unless necessary - he gives worst prices and doesn't level up usefully.

### Flea Market (Level 15 Required)

**Buying Tips**:
- Filter by trader offers (usually cheaper than players)
- Check "require" to see if items need barter
- Sort by price per slot for efficiency
- Use Wish List to track items you need

**Selling Tips**:
- Check current lowest price
- Undercut by small amount for quick sales
- Fee is based on item base value (some items have huge fees)
- High-tier ammo and keys sell fast

**Profitable Items to Flip**:
- Hideout materials (people overpay for convenience)
- Keys (study market prices)
- Weapon attachments during trader restock cooldown

## Quests and Progression

### Quest Types

1. **Elimination**: Kill X enemies with specific conditions
2. **Placement**: Place items at locations (markers, documents)
3. **Collection**: Find and turn in specific items (find in raid)
4. **Exploration**: Visit locations, survive and extract
5. **Skill**: Level up a specific skill to certain level

### Early Game Quest Priority

**Must-Do First**:
1. **"Debut" (Prapor)**: Kill 5 Scavs on Customs
2. **"Checking" (Therapist)**: Get health info from warehouse
3. **"Delivery from the Past" (Prapor)**: Plant folder, get secure docs
   - **IMPORTANT**: This unlocks bigger secure container!
4. **"Shootout Picnic" (Peacekeeper)**: Kill 10 Scavs on Shoreline
   - Unlocks Peacekeeper trader

**Quest Tips**:
- **"Find in Raid" (FiR)** items must be found in current raid and extracted with
- Quest items spawn in specific locations (check wiki)
- Some quests lock out other quests (choose carefully)
- Guides available online for difficult quests

### Important Quest Chains

#### Punisher Series (Prapor)
- Multiple parts requiring specific loadouts
- Unlocks Epsilon container (0.2 bigger than standard)
- Long and difficult but worth it

#### Colleague Series (Therapist)
- Unlock all traders to higher levels
- Essential for progression

#### Gunsmith Series (Mechanic)
- Build specific weapon modifications
- Use wiki/presets to save money
- Unlocks important weapon attachments

### Quest Rewards Priority

**Always complete quests that give**:
- Container upgrades (Epsilon, Kappa)
- Weapon cases / Item cases (storage)
- Permanent skill bonuses
- Rare keys or access items
- +Trader reputation

## Character Development

### Skills System

Skills level through use and provide permanent bonuses.

#### Physical Skills
- **Endurance**: Run/sprint more to level → More stamina
- **Strength**: Carry heavy gear/walk overweight → More carry weight, melee damage, throw distance
- **Vitality**: Take damage and heal → More HP
- **Health**: Survive raids, heal → Overall healing effectiveness
- **Stress Resistance**: Take damage without painkillers → Less tremor when injured

#### Combat Skills
- **Recoil Control**: Fire weapons → Less recoil
- **Assault Rifles/DMR/Shotgun etc.**: Use weapon type → Faster reload, less recoil for that type
- **Mag Drills**: Reload magazines → Faster loading/unloading
- **Attention**: Find loot → Faster looting
- **Perception**: Hear sounds → Better audio range

#### Practical Skills
- **Search**: Search containers → Faster search speed
- **Surgery**: Use CMS/Surv12 kits → Better blacked limb restoration
- **Light/Heavy Vests**: Wear armor → Better movement penalty reduction
- **Metabolism**: Eat/drink → Better energy/hydration bonuses
- **Immunity**: Survive with low health → Better poison resistance

### Hideout

Your personal base with crafting stations and passive bonuses.

#### Essential Hideout Modules (Priority Order)

1. **Medstation Level 1**: Craft basic meds, heal between raids
2. **Lavatory Level 1**: Craft basic items, convert materials
3. **Workbench Level 1**: Craft weapon mods and ammo
4. **Nutrition Unit Level 1**: Passive energy/hydration regeneration
5. **Generator**: Powers everything else
6. **Bitcoin Farm**: Passive income (expensive to build)

**Hideout Benefits**:
- Free healing (saves money on post-raid healing)
- Passive skill leveling (Rest zones)
- Crafting profitable items
- Scav timer reduction
- Stash size upgrades (for Standard edition)

**Resource Management**:
- Keep fuel for generator (Expeditionary fuel tanks)
- Don't waste resources early game
- Focus on money-making stations first
- Bitcoin farm comes later (300M+ roubles investment)

## Survival Tips

### Audio is King

**Sound gives you more information than sight in Tarkov!**

**Listen for**:
- **Footsteps**: Surface type tells you distance/location
- **Gunfire**: Caliber and distance
- **Doors**: Someone nearby looting
- **Looting sounds**: Enemies are distracted
- **Grenades**: Pins pulling, bouncing, explosion
- **Healing**: Enemy is vulnerable
- **Magazines loading**: Enemy reloading

**Your sound management**:
- Walk (default speed) is nearly silent
- Slow crouch is completely silent (alt + W)
- Sprint and jumping are LOUD
- Broken glass crunches loudly
- Different surfaces make different sounds

**Audio Setup**:
- Use headphones (in-game: Comtacs, Razors, Sordins)
- In-game headsets amplify footsteps
- Disable Steam overlay and other sound apps
- Use stereo headphones (not surround - Tarkov uses binaural audio)

### Positioning and Awareness

1. **Always be aware of**:
   - Where you spawned (determines likely enemy spawns)
   - Time elapsed in raid (where players likely are)
   - Common traffic routes
   - Extract locations

2. **Corner checking**:
   - Pie corners (slowly strafe, don't wide peek)
   - Check all angles before entering rooms
   - Use lean (Q/E) to minimize exposure

3. **Use natural cover**:
   - Trees, rocks, vehicles
   - Go prone in bushes (hard to see)
   - Shadows hide you better than light

### Inventory Management in Raid

**Secure Container**:
- Put valuables here during raid (they stay if you die)
- Keys, valuable loot, quest items
- Extra meds and ammo at start
- **CANNOT PUT**: Equipped gear, items found on other players

**Organizing while looting**:
- Sort backpack by value per slot
- Drop lower-value items for better ones
- Tetris items efficiently (rotate with R)
- Use "Discard" to drop items quickly

**Quick looting**:
- Alt + Left Click: Equip item
- Ctrl + Left Click: Move item to container
- Delete key: Discard item
- Check jacket/pockets first (keys, money)

### What to do if...

**...You spawn late?**
- Expect others to be near loot spots already
- Move carefully and listen
- Consider avoiding high-traffic areas

**...You're wounded badly?**
- Find safe spot to heal
- Don't heal in the open
- Prioritize stopping bleeds first
- Pain meds before fighting

**...You hear gunfire?**
- Determine distance and direction
- Avoid area if not quest-related
- Third-party fights after they're weakened
- Stay alert - players attracted to gunfire

**...You're being chased?**
- Break line of sight
- Turn and ambush if you can
- Use grenades to slow pursuit
- Extract if possible

**...Extract is camped?**
- Use alternate extract if available
- Wait and listen before entering extract
- Grenade common camping spots
- Be ready to fight

### Grenade Usage

**Types**:
- **F-1/RGD-5**: Fragmentation grenades (kill/damage)
- **Zarya**: Flashbang (stun enemies)
- **Smoke**: Cover retreat or pushes

**When to use**:
- Clear rooms before entry
- Force enemies out of cover
- Cover your retreat
- Block sight lines with smoke

**Throwing tips**:
- Hold G, adjust power with mouse movement
- Underhand (double tap G) for short distances
- Grenades bounce, be careful indoors
- Cook grenades (hold for 2-3 seconds) to reduce enemy reaction time

## Advanced Strategies

### Scav Karma (Fence Reputation)

**What is it?**
Scav karma affects your Scav cooldown time and starting gear.

**How to gain karma**:
- Kill player Scavs who've killed other Scavs (acting as Scav police)
- Kill PMCs as a Scav
- Extract at friendly Scav+PMC extracts
- Car extracts

**How to lose karma**:
- Kill AI Scavs as player Scav
- Kill friendly player Scavs
- Kill Scav bosses/raiders as Scav

**Benefits of high karma**:
- Shorter Scav cooldown
- Better Scav loadouts
- AI Scavs friendly to you
- Access to Fence's special items

**Recommendation**: Try to keep karma neutral or positive, but don't risk gear for it.

### Ammo/Mag Management

**Bring multiple magazines**:
- Pre-load 3-5 magazines before raid
- Mix ammo (tracers on bottom to know when low)
- Top-load best ammo (first 10 rounds)

**Mag types**:
- High capacity (60-rounders) for suppression
- Standard (30-rounders) for general use
- Fast reload speeds matter in fights

**In-raid reload**:
- Check mag (scroll while reloading shows ammo count)
- Reload before full empty (keep one in chamber)
- Tactical reload keeps chambered round

### Effective Rat vs Chad Playstyles

#### Rat (Sneaky, Loot-Focused)
**Playstyle**:
- Avoid fights when possible
- Hit-and-run tactics
- Cheap gear, valuable loot
- Patience over aggression

**Loadout**:
- Budget weapons (SKS, Mosin, VPO)
- Minimal armor (class 3-4)
- Big backpack
- Quiet movement

**Strategy**:
- Wait for others to fight, loot after
- Use night raids (less players)
- Hit stashes and hidden loot
- Extract early with profit

#### Chad (Aggressive, PvP-Focused)
**Playstyle**:
- Seek fights actively
- Rush high-loot areas
- Best gear, maximum survival
- Aggressive positioning

**Loadout**:
- Meta weapons (M4, HK, Mutant)
- Class 5-6 armor
- Best ammo
- Stims and top-tier meds

**Strategy**:
- Control high-value areas
- Hunt other players
- Fast-paced gameplay
- High risk, high reward

**Most players mix both**: Mid-tier gear, tactical approach, take fights when advantageous.

### Night Raids

**Advantages**:
- Fewer players (less competition)
- NVGs give huge advantage
- Easier quest completion
- Better survival rate

**Disadvantages**:
- Need night vision (expensive)
- Some quests require daytime
- Harder navigation
- Thermals counter your NVG advantage

**Best Night Vision**:
- **Budget**: PN14 or PNV-10T (one-eye, green)
- **Mid-tier**: N-15 (dual tube)
- **High-tier**: GPNVG-18 (quad tube, best FOV)

**Night raid tips**:
- Flashlight gives away position
- Use IR lights (invisible without NVG)
- Move slower and listen more
- AI Scavs have flashlights (easy to spot)

### Team Play

**Communication is essential**:
- Use Discord/TeamSpeak for voice
- Call out positions clearly ("North building, second floor")
- Use compass bearings (0-359 degrees)
- Coordinate equipment (no friendly fire)

**Friendly fire**:
- Tarkov has no indicators for teammates
- Identify before shooting
- Wear armbands (different colors per person)
- Call out your movements

**Team strategies**:
- **Bounding**: One person covers while other moves
- **Room clearing**: Flashbang, then entry team rushes
- **Overwatch**: One sniper, others push
- **Loot sharing**: Distribute based on who needs what

**Team sizes**:
- 2-3 players optimal (communication manageable)
- 4-5 players powerful but chaotic
- Solo is viable and many prefer it

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### 1. **Gear Fear**
**Problem**: Hoarding good gear and never using it
**Solution**: Use your gear! It's meant to be used, not stored. You'll earn more by surviving with better gear.

### 2. **Sprinting Everywhere**
**Problem**: Making tons of noise, alerting everyone
**Solution**: Walk in high-traffic areas, sprint only when safe or rushing.

### 3. **Ignoring Sound**
**Problem**: Not wearing headset or not listening carefully
**Solution**: Always wear in-game headset, use real headphones, pay attention.

### 4. **Looting Too Long**
**Problem**: Staying in one spot too long attracts attention
**Solution**: Loot quickly and move. Don't be greedy, extract when you have value.

### 5. **Fighting Every Battle**
**Problem**: Taking unnecessary fights and dying
**Solution**: Pick your fights strategically. Sometimes running is the best option.

### 6. **Not Learning Maps Offline**
**Problem**: Getting lost, missing extracts
**Solution**: Use offline mode to explore without risk.

### 7. **Using Bad Ammo**
**Problem**: Shooting 60 rounds into armored enemy and doing nothing
**Solution**: Always check ammo pen values. Use good ammo.

### 8. **Bringing Too Much Gear**
**Problem**: Wasting money on excessive meds/ammo you don't use
**Solution**: Bring what you need: 2-3 meds, 3-4 magazines, 1 stack extra ammo.

### 9. **Extract Camping**
**Problem**: Sitting at extract waiting for players (boring and risky)
**Solution**: Play actively. You'll improve more and have more fun.

### 10. **Not Insuring Gear**
**Problem**: Losing everything when you die
**Solution**: Always insure! You get back gear that wasn't looted 30-40% of the time.

### 11. **Selling to Wrong Traders**
**Problem**: Losing money by selling to Fence
**Solution**: Learn which trader pays best for each item type.

### 12. **Ignoring Quests**
**Problem**: Not leveling traders, missing out on progression
**Solution**: Focus on quests early. They unlock better gear and stash space.
