## What is PVE Mode?

PVE (Player vs Environment) mode in Escape from Tarkov allows you to play solo or with friends against AI opponents only - no other human players. This mode is perfect for:
- Learning the game mechanics without PVP pressure
- Practicing maps and extraction routes
- Completing quests at your own pace
- Testing loadouts and strategies

---

## Getting Started

### 1. **Access PVE Mode**
   - Launch Escape from Tarkov
   - Select "PVE" from the main menu
   - Choose your PMC or Scav character
   - Select a map to raid

### 2. **Understanding Your Character**
   - **PMC (Private Military Company)**: Your main character with persistent inventory
   - **Scav (Scavenger)**: Free loadout character with cooldown timer, no risk to your PMC gear

### 3. **Pre-Raid Preparation**
   - Check your gear and ammunition
   - Bring healing items (AI-2, bandages, painkillers)
   - Pack a backpack for loot
   - Know your extraction points before loading in

---

## Basic Gameplay Loop

### The Core Loop:
1. **Enter Raid** → Load into map with gear
2. **Loot & Fight** → Collect items, fight AI Scavs and PMCs
3. **Extract** → Reach extraction point before timer runs out
4. **Survive** → Keep all your loot and gear
5. **Organize & Sell** → Manage inventory, sell to traders
6. **Repeat** → Upgrade gear and complete quests

### If You Die:
- **PMC**: You lose everything you brought into the raid
- **Scav**: No loss, just wait for cooldown to reset

## Beginner Tips

### ✅ **Essential Survival Tips**

1. **Learn One Map First**
   - Start with **Customs** - most beginner quests are here
   - Use offline mode to practice without risk
   - Learn 2-3 extraction points before going online

2. **Manage Your Inventory**
   - Insurance returns items if not taken by players (PVE: less useful)
   - Organize stash using cases and containers
   - Sell junk to traders, keep valuable items

3. **Combat Basics**
   - AI Scavs react to sound - walk slowly near them
   - Headshots kill instantly - aim for the head
   - Use cover and peek corners carefully
   - Painkillers let you run with broken legs

4. **Healing Knowledge**
   - **AI-2 Medkit**: Heals all body parts slowly
   - **Bandages**: Stops bleeding (critical!)
   - **Splints**: Fixes fractures
   - **Painkillers**: Removes pain effects, allows running

5. **Loot Priority**
   - **High Value**: GPUs, Tetriz, Bitcoins, keycards
   - **Medium Value**: Electronics, tools, food
   - **Quest Items**: Always check your active quests
   - **Ammo & Meds**: Essential for survival

### ⚠️ **Common Mistakes to Avoid**

- ❌ Don't run everywhere (makes noise, drains stamina)
- ❌ Don't ignore bleeding (you'll die quickly)
- ❌ Don't extract camp (waiting at extracts is boring in PVE anyway)
- ❌ Don't hoard everything (sell items to level traders)
- ❌ Don't sprint to loot hotspots (AI might be waiting)

### 💰 **Making Money**

1. **Scav Runs** - Free loot with zero risk
2. **Hideout** - Passive income from Bitcoin farm, etc.
3. **Quests** - Unlock better traders and rewards
4. **Loot Routes** - Learn high-value item spawns
5. **Barter Trades** - Some trader barters are very profitable

## What to Do First

### 🎯 **Your First 10 Raids**

**Raids 1-3: Learn the Basics**
- Play **Customs** as Scav
- Find and extract from **Crossroads** or **Trailer Park**
- Don't worry about dying, just learn the map
- Focus on finding the extraction points

**Raids 4-6: Start Looting**
- Loot **Stashes** (buried supply caches, low risk)
- Loot **Duffle bags** and **Jackets** (keys and money)
- Avoid high-traffic areas like Dorms
- Practice shooting AI Scavs

**Raids 7-10: Complete First Quests**
- Accept quests from **Prapor** (first trader)
- Quest: "Debut" - Kill 5 Scavs on Customs
- Quest: "Checking" - Find pocket watch in factory truck
- Quest: "Delivery from the Past" - Place document in dorms
- Start building your quest knowledge

### 📊 **Progression Path**

**Week 1:**
- Level 5-10: Learn Customs, complete early quests
- Unlock Flea Market at Level 15 (can buy/sell with players)

**Week 2:**
- Level 10-20: Branch to Woods and Shoreline
- Focus on trader reputation to unlock better items
- Upgrade hideout basics (Medstation, Workbench)

**Week 3+:**
- Level 20+: Try harder maps (Reserve, Labs)
- Optimize loot runs for profit
- Complete quest lines for trader levels
- Build out full hideout

## Quick Reference Card

### 📋 **Every Raid Checklist**
- [ ] Ammo loaded and spare mags packed
- [ ] At least 1 healing item
- [ ] Painkillers in secure container
- [ ] Know your extract points (double-tap "O" in raid)
- [ ] Check raid time remaining (top right)
- [ ] Insurance applied (optional in PVE)

### ⏱️ **Raid Times**
- Most maps: 40-50 minutes
- Factory: 20 minutes
- Check timer frequently - don't get stuck!

### 🎯 **Key Bindings**
- **Double-tap O**: Check available extracts
- **Tab**: Open inventory
- **Delete**: Discard item
- **Alt + Click**: Quick equip
- **Ctrl + Click**: Quick move to container
