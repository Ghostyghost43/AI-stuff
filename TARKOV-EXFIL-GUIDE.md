## Introduction

This guide provides comprehensive information about extraction points (exfils) for all maps in Escape from Tarkov. Understanding exfils is crucial for survival and successfully extracting with your loot.

### Legend:
- ✅ **Always Available** - Extract is always open
- ⏱️ **Time Limited** - Opens/closes at specific times or has limited availability
- 💰 **Paid** - Requires Roubles to use
- 🔑 **Keyed** - Requires specific key or item
- 🎲 **Random** - Random chance of being available
- 🤝 **Cooperative** - Requires PMC and Scav cooperation
- 🔋 **Power** - Requires power to be activated
- 👥 **Faction Specific** - Only available to certain spawn sides

---

## Exfil Types Explained

### Standard Exfils
Most exfils are standard - you enter the zone, a timer starts (usually 7-10 seconds), and you extract. Stay in the zone until the timer completes.

### Conditional Exfils
Some exfils have special requirements:
- **Paid Exfils**: Costs Roubles (typically 3,000-7,000₽). Money is taken from your inventory.
- **Keyed Exfils**: Requires specific keys or items in your inventory.
- **Shared Exfils**: PMC + Scav cooperation required.
- **Vehicle Exfils**: Paid extracts using vehicles (cars, trains).
- **Status-dependent**: Green flare = available, Red flare/smoke = not available.

### Scav vs PMC Exfils
- PMCs have faction-specific exfils based on spawn location
- Scavs have different exfil options, many overlap with PMC exfils
- Some exfils are Scav-only

## Customs

**Map Size**: Medium | **Difficulty**: Beginner-Friendly | **Raid Time**: 40 minutes

Customs is the most popular beginner map with straightforward exfils. The map is divided by a central bridge area, with most spawns on either the East or West side.

### PMC Extracts

#### USEC Spawns (typically East side):
1. **RUAF Roadblock** 🎲
   - **Location**: Northwest corner, near military checkpoint
   - **Type**: Random availability (30-40% chance)
   - **Status**: Green smoke = available, No smoke = not available
   - **Tips**: Check smoke from distance before committing. High traffic area.
   - **Coordinates**: Northwest corner near Scav checkpoint

2. **Crossroads** ✅
   - **Location**: South-central, near vehicle extract
   - **Type**: Always available for USEC
   - **Tips**: Very open, prone to extract camping. Approach from multiple angles.
   - **Landmarks**: Near the broken concrete barriers and small building

3. **ZB-1013** 🔑
   - **Location**: Northeast in the woods
   - **Type**: Requires Factory Exit Key (not consumed)
   - **Tips**: Safe extract if you have the key. Inside bunker, easy to defend.
   - **Key**: Factory Exit Key (keep in docs case)

#### BEAR Spawns (typically West side):
1. **Trailer Park (Smuggler's Boat)** ✅
   - **Location**: Far southeast, along the river
   - **Type**: Always available for BEAR
   - **Tips**: Long run from most of the map. Check bushes near extract.
   - **Landmarks**: Destroyed boat on the shore

2. **Old Gas Station** ✅
   - **Location**: Southwest corner, at the gas station
   - **Type**: Always available for BEAR
   - **Tips**: Enter the small office building, extract zone inside.
   - **Warning**: Common camping spot, clear before extracting

3. **ZB-1012** 🔑
   - **Location**: Southwest in the woods, near Old Gas
   - **Type**: Requires Factory Exit Key (not consumed)
   - **Tips**: Underground bunker, very safe. Alternative to Old Gas.
   - **Key**: Factory Exit Key

#### Shared Extracts (Both Factions):
1. **Dorms V-Ex (Vehicle)** 💰⏱️
   - **Location**: Between 2-story and 3-story dorms
   - **Type**: Paid (7,000₽), 1 use per raid, may leave without you
   - **Status**: Headlights on = available, will honk when leaving
   - **Tips**: Have exact cash ready. Car waits ~7 minutes then leaves.
   - **Warning**: Extremely dangerous, dorms is high PvP area

2. **Scav Checkpoint** 🤝
   - **Location**: North side, military checkpoint
   - **Type**: Requires PMC + Scav player cooperation
   - **Tips**: Both players must be present. Rarely used.

### Scav Extracts
- **ZB-1011**: North side bunker, always available
- **ZB-1012**: Southwest bunker (if you have Factory key)
- **Administration Gate**: Near admin building, always available
- **Old Gas Station**: Southwest, always available
- **Crossroads**: South-central, always available
- **RUAF Roadblock**: If available (check smoke)
- **Scav Checkpoint**: Scav-only, northeast
- **Factory Shacks**: Near construction area

### Map Tips:
- **Dorms area** = High PvP, valuable loot, risky exfil (V-Ex)
- **Gas station area** = Reshala's spawn, check before extracting
- **RUAF** = Check smoke from far away, don't run there blind
- **Factory Exit Key** = Essential key, opens multiple extracts

## Reserve

**Map Size**: Medium | **Difficulty**: Hard | **Raid Time**: 40 minutes

Reserve is a former military base with complex underground tunnel systems. Features many loot opportunities but challenging navigation and extraction.

### PMC Extracts

#### Standard Extracts:
1. **Bunker Hermetic Door** 🔋
   - **Location**: Underground bunker system, D-2 area
   - **Type**: Requires bunker power to be activated + lever pulled
   - **Tips**: Must turn on power, flip switch on 3rd floor of White Pawn
   - **Process**: Power on → Flip lever (3rd floor) → Extract opens for 4 min
   - **Warning**: Timer based, don't dawdle once opened

2. **Cliff Descent** 🎒
   - **Location**: Cliffs south of the dome
   - **Type**: Requires no backpack (must drop it)
   - **Tips**: Use paracord + Red Rebel ice pick for safer descent
   - **Alternative**: Can do without tools but must drop bag
   - **Items needed**: Red Rebel + Paracord (or just drop bag)

3. **Scav Lands (Beyond Fuel Tanks)** 🎲
   - **Location**: Southwest corner, past the fuel tanks
   - **Type**: Random availability
   - **Status**: Check in-raid if available
   - **Tips**: Long run, crosses open ground

#### Conditional Extracts:
1. **Armored Train** ⏱️💰
   - **Location**: Arrives at railway platform
   - **Type**: Timed, arrives mid-raid, costs 3,000-7,000₽
   - **Status**: Train whistle = arriving, stays ~7 min then leaves
   - **Tips**: Listen for horn, train honks before leaving
   - **Warning**: Everyone knows when it arrives, expect PvP

2. **Sewer Manhole** 🔑
   - **Location**: Inside building, requires specific key
   - **Type**: Keyed extract, opens manhole in building
   - **Key**: Requires RB-MP11 or RB-MP21 key
   - **Tips**: Rare keys, but very safe extract

3. **CP Fence (Checkpoint Fence)** 🤝
   - **Location**: South of map, near checkpoint
   - **Type**: PMC + Scav cooperation required
   - **Tips**: Both factions must be present

### Scav Extracts
- **Scav Lands**: Southwest, past fuel tanks
- **Heating Pipe**: Scav-only, in bunker system
- **Bunker Hermetic Door**: If power activated
- **Cliff Descent**: If you have Red Rebel + Paracord (or no bag)
- **Armored Train**: When it arrives and if you have money
- **CP Fence**: Scav-only or cooperative
- **Depot Hermetic Door**: Underground, requires power

### Map Tips:
- **Gluhar** = Boss with heavy guards, spawns in various locations
- **Raiders** = Triggered by bunker alarm, very dangerous AI
- **Underground** = Complex tunnel system, easy to get lost
- **Red Rebel** = Ice pick tool, allows safe cliff descent (keeps backpack)
- **Raiders** = Can be triggered by hitting bunker alarm
- **Marked Rooms** = Multiple marked rooms, require keys
- **D-2** = Common extract choice, requires power setup

   - **Location**: Central elevator shaft, basement level
   - **Type**: Always available
   - **Tips**: Wait for elevator, exposed while waiting
   - **Warning**: Common camping location

3. **Cargo Elevator** 🔋
   - **Location**: Parking garage area
   - **Type**: Requires power to be turned on
   - **Tips**: Must activate power station first
   - **Process**: Turn on power → Call elevator

#### Special Extracts:

   - **Tips**: Safe but lose backpack

2. **Vent Extract** 🔑
   - **Location**: Ventilation system

### Scav Extracts

- **Colored keycards** = Red, Blue, Green, Violet, Yellow - very expensive

## General Tips & Strategies

### Extract Camping Awareness
**What is Extract Camping?**
Players who wait near extracts to ambush others as they try to leave. This is a controversial but legal tactic.

**High-Risk Extracts for Camping:**
- Customs: Crossroads, RUAF Roadblock, Dorms V-Ex
- Woods: UN Roadblock, Outskirts
- Shoreline: Tunnel, Pier Boat
- Interchange: Emercom, Railway
- Reserve: D-2 Bunker exit
- Factory: Gate 3, Gate 0

**Counter-Camping Tactics:**
1. **Approach carefully** - Never sprint directly to extract
2. **Check angles** - Clear common camping spots before entering
3. **Use grenades** - Pre-nade common hiding spots
4. **Alternative routes** - Approach from unexpected directions
5. **Time awareness** - Most campers arrive 10-15 min before raid end
6. **Listen** - Campers often shift or ADSing makes noise
7. **Use cover** - Move from cover to cover, don't run in the open

### Essential Keys Worth Keeping

**Universal Keys (Multiple Maps):**
- **Factory Exit Key** - Customs (ZB-1012, ZB-1013), Woods (Factory Gate), Factory (Cellars)
- **Red Rebel + Paracord** - Reserve (Cliff Descent), Woods (Cliff), keeps your backpack

**High-Value Keys:**
- **ZB-014** (Woods) - Cheap, safe extract
- **Dorms keys** (Customs) - Marked room, safes, good loot
- **Resort keys** (Shoreline) - East/West wing rooms, medical loot
- **Interchange keys** - Ultra Medical, Kiba, tech stores
- **Reserve keys** - Marked rooms, weapon rooms

### Extract Priority System

**Always Available > Random > Paid > Cooperative**

1. **Always Available (✅)** - Your primary extracts, plan routes to these
2. **Keyed (🔑)** - If you have the key, often safest option
3. **Random (🎲)** - Check early if these are available, adjust plan
4. **Paid (💰)** - Backup option if you have cash
5. **Cooperative (🤝)** - Last resort, rarely works
6. **Power Required (🔋)** - Plan ahead, takes time to activate

### Money Management for Paid Extracts

**Vehicle Extract Costs:**
- Most car extracts: 3,000-7,000₽
- Keep cash in pockets/rig (not backpack/secure container)
- Always carry 10,000₽ as backup for extracts
- Cash is taken directly from inventory when you enter extract

**Which Vehicle Extracts to Use:**
- Use when heavily loaded with valuable loot
- Use when injured and need quick extract
- Use when your normal extract is too far
- DON'T use if you're broke (can't extract if no money!)

### Spawn-Based Extract Planning

**Learn Your Spawns:**
- Each map has spawn zones that determine your faction extracts
- Within 30 seconds of spawning, check your extracts (double-tap O)
- Immediately plan route: Loot → Exit strategy

**Spawn-Side Indicators:**
- **Customs**: East spawn = USEC, West spawn = BEAR (usually)
- **Woods**: South spawn = USEC, North spawn = BEAR
- **Shoreline**: West spawn or East spawn determines extracts
- **Reserve**: All PMCs share most extracts
- **Interchange**: North vs South spawns

### Time Management

**Raid Timers:**
- Factory: 15-20 min (shortest)
- Most maps: 35-45 min
- Streets: 50 min (longest)
- Labs: 35 min (with expensive entry fee)

**Extraction Timing:**
- Leave 5-10 minutes to reach extract safely
- Account for detours, fights, looting on the way
- Extract camping increases in final 10 minutes
- Vehicle extracts may leave before raid ends

**Time-Based Extracts:**
- **Armored Train** (Reserve): Arrives mid-raid, stays ~7 minutes
- **Vehicle extracts**: Typically stay 7-10 minutes then leave
- **Hermetic Door** (Reserve): Opens for only 4 minutes once activated

### Weather and Time of Day

**Night Raids:**
- Bring flashlight, NVGs, or thermal scopes
- Harder to navigate, easier to get lost
- Fewer players (usually), but they're geared
- Some extracts harder to find in the dark
- Factory night raids are 15 min (vs 20 day)

**Day Raids:**
- Better visibility, easier navigation
- More players, higher competition
- Easier to spot enemies at range
- Extract camping more effective

### Scav Extract Strategies

**Scav Advantages:**
- Usually spawn closer to extracts
- Don't lose anything if you die (free raid)
- Can use PMC exfils that are "always available"
- Shorter travel distances generally

**Scav Tips:**
- Check your extracts immediately (double-tap O)
- Scavs have different extract pools than PMCs
- Some Scav extracts are Scav-only
- Don't kill other Scavs (karma penalty, Scavs attack you)
- Loot and leave - don't be greedy

### Emergency Extract Situations

**When Things Go Wrong:**

**Heavily Injured:**
- Prioritize closest extract, even if paid
- Use painkillers to keep moving
- Avoid fights, stealth to extract
- Consider vehicle extracts for speed

**Overweight:**
- Drop items strategically if too slow
- Use SJ6 stims for stamina if you have them
- Plan shorter route to closer extract
- Insurance fraud: Drop insured items, take better loot

**Out of Ammo:**
- Avoid all fights
- Use melee if absolutely necessary
- Take backroads and hidden routes
- Move during gunfights elsewhere (distraction)

**Lost/Disoriented:**
- Check landmarks (sun position, mountains, buildings)
- Open map (if you have one)
- Head to map edges, follow walls to corners
- Use online maps on second monitor/phone

### Extract Loadout Recommendations

**Essential Items for Extracting:**
1. **Painkillers** - Keep moving when injured (ibuprofen, morphine, etc.)
2. **CMS/Surv12 kit** - Heal blacked limbs
3. **Extra ammo** - Don't run dry on the way out
4. **Emergency meds** - IFAK, grizzly, or salewa
5. **Cash** - 10,000₽ minimum for vehicle extracts
6. **Keys** - Factory Exit Key, map-specific keys
7. **Grenades** - Clear extract campers
8. **Flashlight/NVGs** - For dark extracts (tunnels, bunkers)

**Optional but Helpful:**
- **Red Rebel + Paracord** - Reserve/Woods cliff extracts
- **Compass** - Navigation aid
- **Backup weapon** - If primary runs dry
- **Stims** - SJ6 (stamina), propital (health regen)

### Map-Specific Extract Strategies

**Customs (Beginner-Friendly):**
- RUAF is tempting but unreliable (30% spawn)
- Gas station is camped often, clear first
- Dorms car is dangerous but fast
- ZB bunkers are safest with key

**Woods (Navigation-Focused):**
- Learn landmarks, easy to get lost
- UN Roadblock is camped from rocks
- Outskirts has sniper rock, check it
- Don't go for Mountain Stash unless nearby

**Shoreline (Distance Management):**
- Extracts are FAR apart, plan early
- Tunnel is dark, bring light
- Pier boat is very exposed, approach carefully
- Resort fights can make you late for extract

**Interchange (Dark and Dangerous):**
- Very dark inside, bring light source
- Emercom is open, approach from multiple angles
- Railway is long run from mall
- Power plant extract takes setup time

**Reserve (Complex):**
- D-2 bunker requires power + lever pull
- Train is loud, everyone knows it's there
- Red Rebel extract is safest (expensive investment)
- Learn underground system, multiple routes

**Factory (Fast-Paced):**
- Know your extract immediately
- Office window has obvious yellow light
- Gate extracts are camped often
- Cellars is safest with key

**Streets (Large and Complex):**
- Learn landmarks, very easy to get lost
- Multiple car extracts, keep cash ready
- Sewer system is safe but dark
- Allow extra time, map is huge

### Cooperative Extracts (PMC + Scav)

**How They Work:**
- Requires one PMC and one Scav player
- Both enter extract zone together
- Stand in zone for full timer
- Both extract successfully
- Receive rewards (karma, cash, items)

**Locations:**
- Customs: Scav Checkpoint
- Interchange: Scav Camp
- Reserve: CP Fence
- Lighthouse: Various
- Woods: Various

**Reality Check:**
- **Rarely used** - Hard to coordinate with randoms
- **Trust issues** - Either player can betray
- **Communication** - Use voice lines (F1 menu)
- **Risky** - Both players vulnerable while waiting
- **Better with friends** - Coordinate on Discord

### Extract Audio Cues

**Learn These Sounds:**
- **Car engines** - Vehicle extract is running
- **Train horn** - Armored train arriving/leaving (Reserve)
- **Car honk** - Vehicle about to leave (warning)
- **Power hum** - Bunker extract powered (Reserve)
- **Extract music** - Plays when entering extract zone
- **Timer countdown** - Audio cue as timer completes

**Use Audio to Your Advantage:**
- Hear a car? Plan to use it or avoid it
- Train whistle? Expect PvP at railway
- No sounds at vehicle spawn? Extract not available

### Final Survival Tips

1. **Always have an extract plan** - Before looting, know how you're leaving
2. **Don't be greedy** - One more room often leads to death
3. **Trust your instincts** - If extract feels camped, it probably is
4. **Patience wins** - Wait out extract campers if you have time
5. **Insurance works** - Drop insured gear in bushes if you find better
6. **Survival > Loot** - A successful extract with little loot beats dying with millions
7. **Learn one map first** - Master extracts on Customs before moving to others
8. **Use online resources** - Maps, wikis, and guides help tremendously
9. **Practice offline** - Learn extract locations without risk
10. **Stay calm** - Panic leads to mistakes near extracts
